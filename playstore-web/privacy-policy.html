Kotha Bolbo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 August 2026  •  Developer: Md. Abu Sayem

Kotha Bolbo is a messaging app. This policy explains the information used to provide messaging, profiles, notifications, media sharing, and an optional Google Drive backup.

Information we collect and why

  • Account and profile information: Firebase account ID, name, email address, optional phone number, gender, date of birth, bio, profile photo and cover photo. This is used to create and show your account and profile.
  • Messages and media: message text, sender/recipient IDs, timestamps, reply metadata, and any image or voice media you choose to send. This is used to deliver conversations and display chat history.
  • Device and notification information: Firebase Cloud Messaging token and basic notification data. This is used to send message notifications to your device.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: only when you choose a Google account and start/enable backup. The app stores its backup in that account's private Google Drive app-data folder. The current backup contains account/profile metadata and message records, including media links; it does not claim to copy every media binary file.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: Firebase Analytics may process app interaction and device/app information to understand app reliability and usage.

Service providers and sharing

Data is processed using Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging and Analytics), Google Sign-In/Google Drive when you opt in, ImageKit for media hosting, and the app's notification/media API service. We do not sell personal data. We share data only as needed to provide these features, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS. Account and chat data is retained while your account is active unless deleted by you or removed under the app's retention features. Google Drive backups remain in your selected Drive account until you delete them. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can control profile visibility in the app, choose whether to use Google Drive backup, change/delete a Drive backup, and delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete Account. To request deletion through the web, use our Account & Data Deletion page.

Children

Kotha Bolbo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you do not meet the applicable minimum age.
