Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your Kotha Bolbo account inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, email abusayem0866@gmail.com from your registered account email with subject “Kotha Bolbo Account Deletion Request”. Include your registered email and, if available, your profile name. We may request enough information to verify that the account belongs to you.

What is deleted

After verification, we delete the account profile, account authentication record, messages associated with the account, contact records, presence information, and app-hosted profile media. If you have enabled Google Drive backup, first delete it in Settings → Google Drive Backup → Delete Drive Backup. Some data may remain temporarily in provider backups or logs for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations, then be removed according to those providers’ retention processes.

Requests are handled within 30 days unless law or security requirements require a longer retention period.
